{#- Render a master in the browser. WHAT THIS ACTUALLY DOES, stated plainly because the word "mastering" gets used to mean anything: it measures the file to BS.1770-4, works out the gain that lands it on the platform target you chose, caps that gain if it would push the true peak past the ceiling, applies it, and writes a new 24-bit WAV with dither. That is the loudness stage of mastering and nothing else. It does not EQ, compress, or add harmonic character - the Rack does those, with the same engine, and the page says so rather than implying this replaces it. Turning a master down is lossless. Only turning one up can clip, which is why the ceiling check exists and why the result says how much gain was actually applied rather than how much was asked for. -#}
