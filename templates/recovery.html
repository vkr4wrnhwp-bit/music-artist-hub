What this scan can and cannot see
It reads your uploaded rows. Revenue from a source you have never uploaded is invisible to it, and its absence here is not evidence that nothing is missing there. Unattributed is taken straight off the statement. Gap amounts are estimates, weighted by what each silent store paid you. Stores are asked only when you press Check. Deezer, Spotify and Apple Music answer by ISRC; other stores cannot be asked here, and a store nobody asked is never read as missing. The MLC is the one registry it can ask. With the login set, the check above asks per ISRC, and that is the only registration status on this page. It never registers or claims anything for you.
