Adapters declare what they could answer and stay inert until their credentials exist. An adapter that has not been implemented raises rather than returning a plausible guess — a fabricated distributor or manager is worse than a blank one.

Credentials present is not the same as the vendor answering. The state column reads this deployment’s environment; a key can be revoked or a plan lapse without a single variable changing. Test connection spends one real lookup and reports what came back, in the provider’s own words. An adapter with no cheap call to spend says so rather than reporting a pass it did not earn.
