Consent on file: the share of fans with at least one consent record, written at capture or by the list import.

Consent freshness: of the fans who gave their email on a smart link, the share who did so within a year. Imported lists do not carry the date each person agreed, so they are left out rather than read as new.

Deliverability: needs sends. Nothing has been sent from this screen, so it is not measured.

Location coverage: the share whose file carried a city or a country.

Engagement: the share who have done anything on a smart link: a visit, a click, a pre-save.
