Click an instrument band on the screen — Kick, Vocals, Hi-Hats — then use the gold Zone bar above the rack to trim just that range in 1 dB steps; watch its EQ knobs turn as you press. Every unit's ON/OFF kills only that module (knobs hold their positions, export matches), and A/B swaps back to the settings you had before your last tweaks.

Screen. 13 zones mark where each instrument typically lives; the glow is the energy your track has there right now. Honest energy detection — not instrument identification. Solid curve = your EQ; dashed = cab & mic voicing.

SB-01 Harmonic Bank · Tube Stage. Asymmetric waveshaping — the even-harmonic warmth tubes are loved for. An emulation, and labeled as one.

SB-02 Foundation · SUB-1. Not a bass boost: Depth is a deep 45Hz shelf, Shake a 42Hz room resonance, Growl synthesizes sub harmonics so small speakers hear lows they can't play — psychoacoustics, not magic.

SB-03 Tone Bank · EQ-12 Parametric EQ · 12 Band. Drag a knob, double-click resets, scroll trims; Q shapes every band.

SB-06 Master Bus · Center. The classic mid/side trick on any stereo mix — center-panned vocals solo or vanish. Physics, not stem separation; mono files have no sides to keep.

SB-05 / SB-07. The VU needle rides the compressor's real gain reduction; Final Trim is the actual output gain into the peak meter and your WAV export.

SB-08 Cab & Mic Sim AB

Voicings in the style of classic cabinets and mics — modeled curves plus a synthesized room, not measurements of any specific hardware.

SB-09 DLY-1 Delay AB

Feedback echo with a tone filter in the loop — darker repeats, like tape.

REV-1 Reverb AB

A synthesized space through a real convolver — honestly generated, not a sampled hall.

SB-10 Stem Deck

Have your stems? Load them, mute or solo, and bounce the mix through the whole rack. True ML stem separation of a finished mix needs GPU-class models — that's not this, and this page won't pretend it is. Stems stay in this tab; nothing uploads. Center (mid/side) lives on the Master Bus above.

SB-11 Snippet Finder Hooks for Reels · Shorts · TikTok

Scans your track's real energy for the loudest sustained 15- and 30-second stretches — level detection, not taste; trust your ears on the final pick. Preview plays that window, Snippet WAV bounces it through the whole rack, and Vertical video records a 720×1280 WebM with a live spectrum ring (keep this tab visible while it records; phone editors convert WebM to MP4).
