Screen. 13 color strips mark where each instrument typically lives; the glow is the energy your track has there right now. Honest energy detection — not instrument identification. Solid curve = your EQ; dashed = cab & mic voicing.

SB-01 Harmonic Bank · Tube Stage. Asymmetric waveshaping — the even-harmonic warmth tubes are loved for. An emulation, and labeled as one.

SB-02 Procesis Depth · SUB-1 Foundation. Not a bass boost: Depth is a deep 45Hz shelf, Shake a 42Hz room resonance, Growl synthesizes sub harmonics so small speakers hear lows they can't play — psychoacoustics, not magic.

SB-03 Tone Bank · EQ-12 Parametric EQ · 12 Band. Drag a knob, double-click resets, scroll trims. Q on the right shapes every band.

SB-06 Master Bus · Center. The classic mid/side trick on any stereo mix — center-panned vocals solo or vanish. Physics, not stem separation; mono files have no sides to keep.

SB-05 / SB-07. The VU needle rides the compressor's real gain reduction; Final Trim is the actual output gain into the peak meter and your WAV export.

CM-2 Cab & Mic Sim

Voicings in the style of classic cabinets and mics — modeled curves plus a synthesized room, not measurements of any specific hardware.

DLY-1 Delay

Feedback echo with a tone filter in the loop — darker repeats, like tape.

REV-1 Reverb

A synthesized space through a real convolver — honestly generated, not a sampled hall.

SD-8 Stem Deck

Have your stems? Load them, mute or solo, and bounce the mix through the whole rack. True ML stem separation of a finished mix needs GPU-class models — that's not this, and this page won't pretend it is. Stems stay in this tab; nothing uploads.
