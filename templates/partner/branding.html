Your brand

Your artists see this name in the sidebar and on the page where they sign in. On your own domain they see it before they have an account at all.

The colour is checked against the surfaces it sits on before it is saved. One that cannot be read on a dark sidebar is refused, with the contrast it managed. A brand nobody can read is not a brand.

It reaches your wordmark, section headings and labels, the eyebrow on every page band, button edges and the ring that shows a keyboard where it is. Filled buttons take it too, but only when the text on top of it can still be read.

Where you set a logo, your artists see it instead of your name in the sidebar and above the sign-in form. Wide and flat reads best at the size it is drawn.
