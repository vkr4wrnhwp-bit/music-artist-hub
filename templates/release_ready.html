Good to know

What happens to your file

Your upload is kept in private storage, not on a public link. RoEx reads it through a private link that expires.

The report, the previews and the master are made by RoEx. Where RoEx measured a figure, you see RoEx's figure. Where it didn't, the page says "Not measured".

Loudness settings are RoEx's presets (Low, Medium, High), not exact LUFS targets.

Delete an upload any time and our copies of it and its previews go. A master you bought stays until you delete it.
