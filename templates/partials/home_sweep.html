Royalty Sweep · free to run

The money that never arrived.

Royalties go missing quietly. A recording registered under a misspelled name. A composition with no publisher attached. A source that reported for three quarters and then stopped. Nothing announces itself — it just doesn’t show up on the statement.

The Sweep reads your catalog and your statements, and returns the gaps as cases you can act on.

Sweep my catalog What it looks at
01

Detect

Missing registrations, metadata conflicts, duplicate records, unexplained reporting gaps.

02

Match

Recordings against compositions, contributors against identifiers, ownership against source.

03

Verify

Evidence reviewed, ownership confirmed, and the missing paperwork named before anything moves.

04

Claim

The correction, inquiry or recovery case prepared and filed under your approval.

05

Track

Progress, requests for information, resolutions, and income actually recovered.

Nothing is filed without your sign-off. A finding is a lead, not a claim — and the Sweep says which is which.
