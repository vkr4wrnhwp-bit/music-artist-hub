Built for artist control

You own the work

Masters, catalog, creative assets and release information stay under your control, per your agreement.

Your data stays yours

Artist data is not sold. Music and imagery are not used for AI training without explicit permission.

The AI shows its working

Every recommendation carries its observations, its confidence, and what it still doesn’t know.

Findings are evidenced

The Sweep surfaces potential gaps. Each one needs proof and your approval before it is submitted.
