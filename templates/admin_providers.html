Working means the service answered a real, read-only question when you last checked. Failing means it was asked and refused or did not answer, in its own words. Not asked means nothing was sent: no safe check exists, an owner ruling keeps it off, or it has not been checked yet. That is not a failure. A known state a service reported when asked, such as Google Routes not being switched on for the project, is named on its row and is not a failure either.
